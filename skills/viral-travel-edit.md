# Viral travel edit — structure that works

Target 45–90 s for social, 60–75 s sweet spot. Horizontal 16:9 (YouTube) with 2.39:1 bars for the
cinematic feel. Every cut lands on a beat.

## Timeline (68 s @120 BPM example — the NYC vlog)
| time | section | content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | intro: **hook** | most dynamic/iconic moment + big text "48 HOURS IN NYC" |
| 3–6 s | intro: teasers | 3 × 1 s flashes from different days/places |
| 6–10 s | **title shot** | stable, iconic landmark, slow push-in, "welcome to NEW YORK" |
| 10–16 s | build | journey/transit/people; cuts accelerate 2s → 0.5s into the drop |
| 16–32 s | **drop** | flash + impact; best energetic shots, 1 s cuts, location tags |
| 32–40 s | break | breathe: golden water, silhouettes, slow 2 s scenic shots, more ambience |
| 40–44 s | build 2 | "DAY 02" chapter, streets, fast cuts |
| 44–60 s | **drop 2** | second set of highlights |
| 60–68 s | outro | calm wide beauty shots; end card "NEW YORK, you were a vibe." |

## Shot selection
- Hook: motion + subject + place in one frame (ferry approaching the statue, a reveal, a jump).
- Title: wide and stable for 4 s; leave headroom (titles + letterbox eat the top/bottom 13 %).
- Story stays chronological — viewers feel the journey. Hook/teasers can come from anywhere.
- Alternate wide / medium / detail; people ↔ place. Never two near-identical shots back to back.
- Skip the first ~2 s of phone clips (auto-exposure is still settling → blown-out frames).
- Kids, smiles, waves, iconic poses = gold. Put them on drops.
- Closer: wide, calm, golden light, water/skyline reflections.

## Motion
- Every shot gets a subtle push-in or pull-out (2–10 %) so nothing feels static.
- 1-beat shots get a stronger punch-in (5–13 %).
- Speed 1.5–2× only for slow tilts/reveals that would otherwise be too slow.

## Text
Max 5 overlays + location tags. Hook ≤ 22 chars. Main title 1–2 words. Location tags only at the
first shot of a new place. Chapter cards ("DAY 02") only for multi-day trips.
